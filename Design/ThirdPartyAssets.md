# Third-party brand assets

## Google sign-in buttons

The light and dark rectangular iOS sign-in button PNGs in `Assets.xcassets/GoogleSignInLight.imageset` and `GoogleSignInDark.imageset` are unmodified pre-approved assets from Google's [Sign in with Google branding downloads](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip). They are used under the [Google Identity branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines), checked 2026-10-09 (page last updated 2026-07-07).

Keep each button intact and preserve its aspect ratio. Do not recolor, crop, cover, or redraw the Google mark, or use it to imply Google endorses Veil. If the button treatment changes, recheck the current guidelines and use an approved asset or obtain the required written permission. Google sign-in remains subject to the app's existing provider configuration and capability gates; adding these assets does not register or activate a Google provider.
