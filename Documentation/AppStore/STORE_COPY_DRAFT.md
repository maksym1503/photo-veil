## Description draft

Share the moment. Keep the details.

Veil helps you hide sensitive details before sharing a photo. Choose an image, blur what you want to keep private, and export a new copy.

• Blur backgrounds while keeping the foreground in focus.
• Automatically blur detected faces and likely license plates; tap each region to toggle it.
• Select precise areas with a rectangle or paint them with the freehand brush.
• Adjust blur strength, zoom in to check details, and undo edits.
• Review a clean preview before opening the iPhone share sheet.

Photo processing happens on your iPhone. No login, analytics or Veil server uploads. Your original stays unchanged.

Detection can miss details. Always review your photo before sharing; blur does not guarantee anonymity.
