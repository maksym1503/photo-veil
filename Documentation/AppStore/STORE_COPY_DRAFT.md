## Description draft

Your photo. Your boundaries.

Veil prepares photos for safer sharing with three clear tools: blur and redaction, metadata control, and custom text watermarks. Hide visible details, decide which source photo information travels, and give your copy a purpose. Your original stays unchanged.

After Done, choose **Remove metadata** or **Keep metadata** before saving or sharing. Remove omits source location, capture date/time, camera/device details, descriptive metadata, comments and embedded previews. Keep preserves supported source details, including location when available. JPEG re-encoding cannot retain every tag: original previews, auxiliary images, opaque application data and unsupported comments are excluded; orientation, dimensions and color information are rebuilt. The choice follows Save to Photos, Share, the saved gallery copy and future cloud sync, including thumbnails. Originals stay unchanged. Basic image-format information remains, and Photos or receiving apps may add dates or identifiers.

- Soften a background while keeping the subject in focus.
- Find faces and likely number plates, then review which areas to hide.
- Cover document details or the whole document.
- Mark any area with a rectangle, ellipse or freehand brush.
- Choose Blur, Pixelate or solid Redact, with separate settings for each tool.
- Zoom in, refine your choices and undo edits before reviewing the final photo.
- Choose whether to remove source metadata or keep supported photo details.
- Add a privacy-minded or custom text watermark; drag, style, rotate or repeat it.
- Share, save to Photos, or keep finished photos in your Private Gallery.

Explore every editing tool free. Your first two successful exports are included without registration. Veil Pro unlocks unlimited protected photo exports with a monthly or yearly subscription. Eligible users can start a three-day trial; subscriptions renew automatically at the displayed price until cancelled.

Detection and editing happen on your iPhone. Local editing does not require a Veil account. Veil adds no analytics or tracking. Detection can miss details: always review the result. Blur and pixelation do not guarantee anonymity. A watermark communicates intended use, but cannot prevent removal or misuse. Custom watermark text stays in the editing session until you save or share it as visible pixels. Operational dates and identifiers remain in Veil's gallery/cloud records; removal of embedded image details does not remove those records.

For a configured and validated account/cloud release only, add: “Optionally sign in with Apple or Google and choose whether to sync your processed gallery photos. Originals and recognized text are never uploaded. Cloud storage is not end-to-end encrypted.” Omit this paragraph from an unconfigured release.

## App Review notes — finalize for the selected release

Veil is an iPhone photo privacy utility. The default build requires no login or purchase. Choose a photo using the system picker, then Background, Faces, Plates, Documents or Manual. Tap detected regions to change selection. Manual supports rectangle, ellipse and brush. Tools can remain applied together, with independent effect settings. Done opens a review with Remove metadata / Keep metadata; Share creates a new JPEG, Save to Photos requests add-only permission on invocation, and Save to Veil keeps a protected local output. No broad photo-library read permission is requested. The original remains unchanged. Detection is fallible and runs locally with Apple Vision/Core Image.
