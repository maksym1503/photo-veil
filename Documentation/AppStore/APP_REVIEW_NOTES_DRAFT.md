# App Review notes — local-only release draft

**Draft only.** Provisional factual notes are now saved/read back in the version-1.0 App Review record through Apple’s API, without contact values or submission. Update these notes against the final submitted build after the
owner has published and configured the real Privacy Policy URL and entered a
monitored App Review contact in App Store Connect. Do not put contact values or
credentials in this file.

Veil edits a new image copy on the iPhone. No reviewer account or login is
needed, and account sign-in/cloud sync are not configured in this release.

To review the local workflow:

1. Choose a photo with Apple's system photo picker.
2. Try Redact (including Manual), Metadata (Keep or Remove), and Watermark.
3. Tap Done, review the new copy and metadata choice, then use Share, Save to
   Photos, or Save to Veil. The selected original remains unchanged.
4. Open Private Gallery to inspect and delete saved processed copies.

Detection is fallible; suggestions may miss details or include unrelated areas.
Inspect each result. Blur and pixelation do not guarantee anonymity.

The first two successful Save/Share operations are included. Failed/cancelled
outputs do not count. In this candidate configuration, Veil Pro purchases are
disabled until a real public Privacy Policy URL is configured. Do not test or
advertise purchase completion from this configuration. If that URL is
configured before review, re-check the final build's StoreKit products,
localized prices and eligible three-day trial state, then update these notes.

App Review contact is a required owner-supplied field in App Store Connect;
none is supplied in the repository. No test credentials are required for the
local photo-editing workflow.
