Veil processes selected photos on your iPhone to blur backgrounds, faces, likely plates and manually selected regions. Photo processing happens entirely on-device. Photos are not uploaded to Veil servers.

Veil receives the photo you choose through Apple's system photo picker. It does not request general access to your library, create accounts, run advertising, use analytics or track you. We do not collect personal information through the app.

The selected photo and edits remain in app memory while the session is open. Export produces a new JPEG in app temporary storage and presents the system share sheet. Temporary files may remain until the system removes them; the app is not a photo archive. Your original remains unchanged. The exported JPEG does not copy the original's EXIF/GPS metadata.

Apple's photo picker may retrieve a photo stored in iCloud. If you share an export with another app or service, that recipient handles the copy under its own privacy policy. Apple may provide diagnostics or store reporting according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies; Veil has no custom telemetry service.

Automatic detection may miss private details. Blur does not guarantee anonymity or removal of all identifying information. Review the whole exported photo before sharing.
