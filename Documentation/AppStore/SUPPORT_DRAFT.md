Choose a photo, select Background, Faces, Plates or Manual, review with Done and use Export to share a new JPEG. Tap detected faces/plates to toggle their blur. In Manual, use rectangle or brush; pinch to zoom and switch to Move photo for panning. Undo reverses the previous edit.

If detection misses a detail, use Manual. If Background cannot identify a subject, use the manual fallback. Always inspect the final photo.

If a photo cannot open or export, try another image and report the iPhone model, iOS version, Veil version/build and steps that failed. **Do not send sensitive photos by default.** The developer must confirm how support messages and voluntarily supplied attachments are retained before publishing this page.

Processing occurs on-device; there is no Veil account or server photo library.
