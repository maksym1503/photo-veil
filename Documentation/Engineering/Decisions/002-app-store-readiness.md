# ADR-002: Inactive subscriptions and local-first release preparation

Status: accepted for reversible preparation; quota/commercial activation remains proposed
Date: 2026-10-09 · CTO: gpt-6-astra (low) · Decision authority: Lead

- **Problem:** V4 needs polished product surfaces and a future monthly freemium offering without provisioning services or changing approved local editing access.
- **Constraints:** Preserve optional accounts, explicit cloud consent, rendering and test architecture, owner signing settings and README's V4 acceptance gate. No production credentials, payments, providers or distribution.
- **Alternatives:** No change leaves readiness gaps. Native StoreKit 2 preparation adds no billing dependency. A third-party billing service adds unnecessary cost/processors. Enforcing one free photo now would prematurely settle a tentative product decision.
- **Decision:** Separate StoreKit entitlement/purchase UI from account and editor. Missing/invalid commerce configuration performs no StoreKit work and leaves V4 unrestricted. Prepare a pure allowance decision model, with no usage persistence or enforcement. Public subscription entry requires explicit valid configuration and real legal links. DEBUG preview cannot purchase. Use verified allowlisted current entitlements, transaction updates, foreground refresh, user-initiated restore, localized monthly pricing and management.
- **Startup:** Home renders immediately; retain coalesced off-main gallery opening before account restoration reads consent. Partition temporary exports by process session before moving prior-session cleanup off the main thread; cleanup must never remove the active session. No artificial splash delay. Add native launch artwork and diagnostics without photo/account payloads.
- **Accounts/privacy:** Retain existing native Apple and Google PKCE adapters, secure storage and account deletion. No sign-in requirement for local use or Apple purchases. Present providers together only when Apple capability is enabled; real provider readiness is an owner verification gate. Do not publish invented policy URLs, support contacts or retention commitments.
- **Risks:** Local-only quota would reset on reinstall; gallery count cannot represent usage. StoreKit compilation is not sandbox/payment acceptance. Legal drafts are not published policies. Startup improvements require physical cold-launch measurements before making performance claims.
- **Future proposal:** One first successful export per editing session consumes one installation-local allowance; additional export destinations in that session are free. Never charge for selection/detection/failed saves. Share cancellation and crash accounting need owner-approved semantics. Do not store source fingerprints or introduce cross-device identity to prevent resets without a privacy decision.
- **Cost/reversibility:** Native frameworks only; no external activation. Prepared code/configuration and artwork are reversible. No schema/migration change.
- **Acceptance:** Deterministic configuration/allowance and old-versus-active export cleanup tests; existing core and native PR UI plan; targeted Settings/account integration and visual evidence. Reviewer independently inspects diff, followed by QA. Preserve real-device/backend release gates.
- **CEO approval required later:** Free allowance and usage semantics, paid benefits/ongoing value, price/territories/product IDs and activation, legal identity/contact/URLs/retention, provider setup/signing and distribution. No owner approval is needed for this inactive preparation.
- **Decision evidence:** Read-only CTO consultation `/root/architecture`; technical preparation accepted by Lead. No commercial or release approval inferred.
