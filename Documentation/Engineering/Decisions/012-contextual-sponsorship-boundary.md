# ADR-012 — Contextual home sponsorship and public legal links

Accepted 2026-10-27 after CTO review, under the owner's focused advertising/internal-upload instruction. Preserve the two successful free exports, verified Apple subscriptions, editing and default Keep metadata. No provider registration, paid service, agreement acceptance, public legal publication or final App Review.

Use native-rendered contextual text creatives with an injectable, cancellation-safe provider. No in-process ad SDK or remote executable/web content: Google non-personalized ads still collect data, and an SDK cannot be proven unable to inspect app memory merely by omitting photo arguments. No new backend. A future approved inventory endpoint gets only a fixed home placement; no photo, metadata, OCR, account, export count, edit activity or transaction payload. Ephemeral URLSession, bounded response, strict public HTTPS URLs, no cookies/cache/identifiers or measurement calls. Report/why-this-ad controls and explicit session consent are required. Refusal/unavailable consent means no ad. No ATT request or behavioral targeting.

Missing approved provider configuration, published Privacy/Support URLs or reviewed disclosure means a disabled adapter, no network, prompts, placeholder ads or blank space. Live advertising remains pending actual provider/contract/data-flow and legal review; no invented credentials. The current no-collection manifest remains correct only for this disabled, SDK-free configuration. A configured-ad Release must fail validation until disclosures are reviewed. Disclose dormant preparation to App Review rather than remotely enabling it.

Only an empty, foreground home may contain a small banner separated from import actions. Editor, picker, gallery, guide, settings, paywall, export, background, purchase/restore, unknown/loading/failed verification are ad-free. Pro (including trial/grace) is ad-free. A false initial subscribed flag is not evidence of free access: require a completed verified entitlement/status/catalog resolution; stale loads are discarded on every gate change. This presentation gate never grants or revokes export access.

Public legal links come from validated build-time Info.plist HTTPS values; Apple Standard EULA remains. No URL is invented and a local explanation is not a published policy. Keep purchasing gated until the owner supplies the approved published policy. Existing internal TestFlight authorization applies after checks; no public distribution.

Validate eligibility, consent/version/URLs, failures and stale responses with deterministic core tests; native trial/restore ad suppression, export regression, hero animation/Reduce Motion and screenshots; Release resource/privacy isolation and signed upload readback. Retain historical failures and existing README/V4 owner acceptance gate. No timeout inflation or unchanged retries.

The owner subsequently set build5 usesNonExemptEncryption=false and enabled internal testing (live readback2026-10-27). CTO approved carrying that existing Apple-system-only classification into build6 Info.plist, contingent on unchanged final crypto/dependency inventory. This is not a new encryption decision. Preserve owner-created internal groups; no external distribution.
